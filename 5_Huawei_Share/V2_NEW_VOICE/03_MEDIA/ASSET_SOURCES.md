It was downloaded on 2026-10-25 into `../../03_MEDIA/raw/` and extracted to `../../03_MEDIA/frames/`; this edit reads those frames unchanged. On screen it is credited «فيديو رسمي · HUAWEI <model>». No watermark or attribution was removed, no AI-generated footage was used, and nothing was bought.

**No new footage was downloaded.** The three official films already give three distinct, readable demonstrations of the closing hand at the sender, the opening hand at the receiver, and the receiving screen responding. They cover every footage beat of the new narration.

**Freeze / close-up.** This is a still of `m70_grab` (frame 27), shown desaturated and zoomed with a dashed ring. It is clearly a paused frame of the official film, not a new shot.

**Annotations on footage.** The pulse rings, viewfinder brackets and gesture tags are editorial graphics (badge «تمييز توضيحي»). They are not Huawei UI.

**Explanatory graphics** are labelled «رسم توضيحي» and made of paper cut-out phones, hands, buttons, the Wi-Fi arc and the cursor.
- **System approvals:** shown only as a stamp «موافقات النظام». No permission screen was invented.
- **AirDrop:** shown as a *similar idea* only («فكرة شبيهة بـ AirDrop» with «AirDrop ميزة مشاركة من Apple · تشبيه للفكرة فقط»). No Apple logo is used, and there is no claim that Huawei uses AirDrop.
- **The hand is never drawn storing or carrying data.** The "through the hand" route is shown only crossed out.

**Music and SFX** are original synthesis (`_BRAND_SYSTEM/tools/sfxlib.py` + `04_PRODUCTION/audio/audio_design.py`). No third-party audio was used.
