## How the changed ranges were measured
Every frame of V1 was compared with the same frame of V2 by PSNR (`qc/psnr_v1_v2.log`). Frames below 40 dB count as changed.
- **Changed:** 290 frames, about 16 % of the runtime, all inside the planned windows.
- **Unchanged:** 1495 frames, about 84 %. They match V1 at 40.6–55 dB (mean 48.2 dB), which is re-encode noise only.
