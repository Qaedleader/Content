# Asset manifest — Video 12 «How to be millionaire with AI»

All external media come from Wikimedia Commons. They were downloaded by `scripts/fetch.py`, which writes a licence sidecar JSON beside each original in `raw/commons/`. The originals are untouched; render copies were made by `scripts/prep_media.py` and live in `_BRAND_SYSTEM/public/episodes/millionaire/media`. Every piece of real media is shown on screen with a credit tag. None of it is watermarked, and none of it contains a stock model or a random person's face.
