## Rules applied
- Only free licences were used (CC0, PD, CC BY and CC BY-SA). Every real image carries an on-screen credit tag naming its author and licence.
- No random people. The rolling-mill photo contains distant, unidentifiable workers; there are no faces.
- **ShareAlike:** the CC BY-SA assets carry the same commercial-use note as V1 (`../LICENSES.md` §4). No legal review was performed.
- **Original illustration:** all paper machinery, maps, boards and icons are original vector illustrations from the brand system.
