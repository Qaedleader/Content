## Notes
- **Sam Altman photos:** CC BY 2.0 (Steve Jurvetson; TechCrunch; Village Global). No interview footage was used — the Cheeky Pint video is on YouTube with no reuse licence, so it was **not downloaded**; the remark is shown as a typeset verbatim quote with its source.
- **No fabricated quotes/lip-sync:** the narrator's «لا تبيعوا هذه القطعة للناس العاديين…» line is labelled on screen «تفسير الراوي لكلامه · مش اقتباس حرفي» and «صياغة الراوي · لم يقلها ألتمان بهذا النص».
- **Logos** (OpenAI, ChatGPT, Samsung, SK hynix, Micron: public-domain text/simple logos on Commons; NVIDIA: Apache 2.0 file on Commons) remain **trademarks**, used nominatively to identify the companies.
- **CC BY-SA** images were cropped/graded (adaptation) → share-alike applies to those adapted images. The gold RAM is a colour-graded derivative of a CC BY 4.0 photo (credited, labelled «التذهيب معالجة بصرية»).
- **Videos with burned-in text overlays were cut around** (ST clips: only overlay-free ranges; the overlay-heavy ranges were rendered then discarded). Clip ranges containing people walking into frame were trimmed out (no random people).
- **Downloaded but not used:** H100 photos (one has a price overlay), P100 / Vega HBM die photos, DRAM die shots, Mostek 64K RAM, Pleiades/JUPITER/Frontier extra stills, MRDIMM, wafer, gold ingot, several clips (`v_wafer`, `v_st_chips`, `v_st_wafer`, `v_fab_y2`). The 3 GB "Nvidia DGX Station" video was downloaded by mistake and deleted (unused; shows trade-show visitors).
- **Not used / not obtainable:** Pexels (blocks automated access), official Samsung/SK hynix/Micron/NVIDIA press media (no clear reuse terms checked in this session), YouTube footage (no reuse right).
- **Music & SFX:** original numpy synthesis in `04_PRODUCTION/audio/audio_design.py` + `_BRAND_SYSTEM/tools/sfxlib.py` — no third-party audio.
- **Fonts:** brand fonts (Alexandria, Baloo Bhaijaan 2, Lalezar, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, JetBrains Mono) — all SIL OFL.
