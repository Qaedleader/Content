## Processing

- **Frame rate:** sources are 25 fps and are converted to 30 fps by frame repetition. There is no interpolation and no speed change.
- **Scaling:** frames are scaled down to 1160×870 and shown in a 968×718 print window, so nothing is upscaled.
- **Crops:** every crop keeps both the hand and the screen. Shots with actors' faces were excluded: the Mate 70 woman-at-the-shelf intro, the Mate 80 woman at the bar, and the Pura 80 people on the sofa.

## Explanatory graphics (labelled «رسم توضيحي» where they first appear)

- Paper cut-out phones, hands, the «إرسال»/«استقبال» buttons, the wireless arcs, and the Bluetooth / Wi-Fi glyphs.
- **System approvals:** shown only as a stamp «موافقات النظام ✓». No permission dialog was invented.
- **AirDrop:** shown as a *similar idea* («فكرة شبيهة بـ AirDrop»), with the label «AirDrop: ميزة مشاركة من Apple». The Bluetooth-discovery and Wi-Fi Direct-transfer steps are presented as the general principle, with the note «مقارنة بالمبدأ العام · التفاصيل التقنية تختلف حسب الجهاز والإصدار». No Apple logo is used.
- **Ending:** the ending frames the idea as «تصميم تفاعل ذكي» (clever interaction design). It makes no security or scam claim.
